Publications & Preprints

A finite element method using a bounded auxiliary variable for solving the Richards equation

Accepted (2026)
Benfanich, A., Bourgault, Y., & Beljadid, A. (2026) · Journal of Computational Physics

Introduces a finite element formulation with a bounded auxiliary variable to handle dry and saturated zones with structure-preserving guarantees.

DOI ScienceDirect arXiv Preprint PDF

Mathematical analysis for a doubly degenerate parabolic equation: Application to the Richards equation

Under Review
Benfanich, A., Bourgault, Y., & Beljadid, A. (2026) · IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis (arXiv:2602.19037)

Establishes rigorous mathematical analysis, well-posedness, and stability results for a doubly degenerate parabolic model arising from Richards infiltration dynamics.

Discrete positivity and maximum principles for a finite element discretization of the Richards equation

Under Review
Benfanich, A., Bourgault, Y., & Beljadid, A. (2026) · Journal of Computation and Applied Mathematics (arXiv:2605.09615)

Develops a semi-implicit finite element framework with a bounded auxiliary variable and proves discrete positivity and maximum principle conditions for Richards-type discretizations.
